Questions homeowners ask

What does a home watch service do?

A home watch professional visits your empty home on a schedule, inspects it inside and out, sends you a dated report with photos and calls you if something needs attention.

How much does home watch cost?

Weekly visits for an average home commonly run about $100–$250 a month, depending on market and home size. Try the free cost estimator for your home.

Is Home.Watch free for homeowners?

Yes. Matching, tools, guides and alerts are free for homeowners. Providers pay to be listed and to receive requests.

Are providers vetted?

We ask every listed provider to confirm insurance, bonding and background checks and we encourage you to verify certificates before hiring. See our checklist of questions to ask.

Which areas do you cover?

We accept requests across the United States and Canada, with in-depth guides for Florida, Arizona, Texas, California, the Carolinas, mountain towns, lake country and the Northeast coast.
