How to Choose a Home Watch Provider: 21 Questions to Ask

The questions that separate professional home watch companies from casual helpers: insurance, bonding, background checks, reports, response times and contracts.

By the Home.Watch editorial team. Updated Nov 18, 2026. 3 min read.

On this page

  1. Credentials and protection
  2. The visit itself
  3. Reports and communication
  4. Emergencies
  5. Contract and money
  6. Red flags
  7. Next steps

You are handing a stranger your keys, your alarm code and responsibility for one of your largest assets. A good home watch professional welcomes hard questions. Use this list on every provider you consider, and get the important answers in writing.

Credentials and protection

  1. Are you a registered business in this state or province? Can you share your business license?
  2. Do you carry general liability insurance, and what are the limits? Ask for a certificate of insurance naming you as certificate holder.
  3. Are you bonded? A fidelity bond protects you against theft by the provider or staff.
  4. Has everyone who will enter my home passed a criminal background check?
  5. Do you hold any industry training or accreditation? Ask what it required.
  6. Do you carry workers' compensation if you have employees?

The visit itself

  1. What is on your interior and exterior checklist? Ask for a copy.
  2. How long does a typical visit take for a home like mine?
  3. Will the same person visit each time?
  4. Do you run water, flush toilets and check under sinks on every visit?
  5. How do you check humidity and temperature? Do you record readings?
  6. Will you vary visit times so the house does not show a predictable pattern?

Reports and communication

  1. How is each report delivered and how quickly after the visit?
  2. Do reports include timestamps and photos?
  3. Who do you call first if you find a problem, and what can you authorize without me?
  4. Can my reports be shared with my insurance agent if needed?

Emergencies

  1. What is your response time if I get a leak or temperature alert between visits?
  2. Do you have relationships with plumbers, HVAC techs and restoration companies?
  3. What is your plan for hurricanes, ice storms or wildfires?

Contract and money

  1. What is included in the monthly price, what costs extra, and how are extras approved?
  2. What is the contract term, and how do I cancel or pause service?

Red flags

  • No written checklist or sample report
  • Refuses to provide a certificate of insurance
  • Cash only, no contract, no invoices
  • Cannot name the vendors they use in an emergency
  • Pressure to sign a long contract before a walk-through

Next steps

Shortlist two or three providers and ask each for a free walk-through. Use the Visit Report Builder as your benchmark of what a complete visit covers. Or let us match you with home watch pros and compare them side by side.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between insured and bonded?

Liability insurance pays for damage the provider causes. A fidelity or janitorial bond covers theft by the provider's employees. A professional should carry both.

Should I hire a neighbor instead?

A trusted neighbor is better than nobody, but neighbors rarely carry insurance, keep written reports or follow a checklist, and they may not be available when you need them.
