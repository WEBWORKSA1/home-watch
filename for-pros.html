Why list with us

  • Qualified requests. Every request includes location, home size, schedule and services — so you quote with confidence.
  • Seasonal timing. Departure reminders bring homeowners to the site weeks before they leave, when they're ready to book.
  • Trust signals. Show insurance, bonding, background checks and any accreditation on your profile.
  • Free tools. Use the Visit Report Builder and our guides with your own clients.

Who we look for

Registered home watch, property care and absentee-owner service businesses in the United States and Canada that carry general liability insurance and a fidelity bond, and background-check everyone who enters a client's home.
