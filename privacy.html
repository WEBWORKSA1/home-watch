Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Home.Watch ("we", "us") collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to home.watch and its pages.

Information you give us

When you use a form — a match request, provider application, contact message, newsletter or reminder signup, contest entry, job application, sponsorship inquiry or support pledge — we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, property location and the information about your home and schedule you choose to share.

Form submissions are transmitted through FormSubmit, a third-party form-processing service, which delivers them to our private inbox. Please do not include sensitive information such as alarm codes, account numbers or government ID numbers in any form.

How we use it

  • To answer your message or request
  • To match you with home watch providers, if you ask us to
  • To send reminders and newsletters you sign up for (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To administer contests, applications and sponsorships
  • To keep the site secure and improve it

When we share it

If you submit a match request, we share the details of your request with up to three independent home watch providers who cover your area, so they can contact you. Those providers handle your information under their own privacy practices. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users, or as part of a business transfer.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info or, in Canada, youradchoices.ca. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, consent for personalized ads is requested through a consent message before such cookies are used.

Cookies and browser storage

We use a small amount of browser storage to remember your theme, cookie choice, checklist ticks and report drafts. These stay on your device. See our cookie policy.

Third-party content

Videos are embedded from YouTube only after you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Weather data is requested from the National Weather Service when you use the weather tools. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25 — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, to withdraw consent and to object to certain processing. To make a request, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". We will respond within the time required by law.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

Home.Watch is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
