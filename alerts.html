Freeze and heat board

Seven-day lows and highs for second-home markets and the northern homes snowbirds leave behind. Freeze means a forecast low at or below 32°F (0°C); hard freeze means 20°F (−7°C) or below; heat means a high of 100°F (38°C) or more. Updated daily. Forecast data: MET Norway (CC BY 4.0).
