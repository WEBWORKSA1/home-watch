Home Watch on the Texas Gulf Coast: Heat, Humidity and Hurricanes

Seasonal home care on the Texas coast and in the Rio Grande Valley: humidity, hurricanes, freezes, salt air and home watch planning.

The Rio Grande Valley and the Texas coast draw large numbers of winter visitors. Homes left empty from spring to fall face heat, humidity and the Gulf hurricane season, while rare winter freezes can catch homes built without much freeze protection.

Markets covered: South Padre Island, McAllen, Harlingen, Brownsville, Corpus Christi, Rockport, Port Aransas, Galveston.

Seasonal risk calendar

WhenWhat to watch
Nov–MarPeak winter-visitor season. Rare hard freezes can burst exposed pipes in homes not built for cold.
Apr–MayDepartures. Set humidity control and arrange storm preparation.
Jun–NovGulf hurricane season, with peak activity August through October. Heat and humidity stress air conditioners.

How often should a home watcher visit?

Weekly visits during summer are common on the coast. Inland Valley homes sometimes use every-other-week visits with remote humidity monitoring.

Add these to your checklist

  • Humidity under 60% and AC set point confirmed
  • Salt-air corrosion checks on exterior equipment
  • Storm-prep authorization and post-storm inspection
  • Exposed exterior pipes insulated for rare freezes
  • RV, golf cart and vehicle checks for park-model residents
