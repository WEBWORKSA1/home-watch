    What the score means

    The score adds up the factors behind the most common and costly losses in empty homes: undetected water leaks, freezes, humidity, break-ins and gaps in insurance conditions. Under 30 is low risk, 30–59 is moderate and 60 or more is high. The fixes are ordered by how many points each one would remove.

    It is a planning aid, not an inspection. A home watch professional or contractor can assess your property in person. Learn more in what home watch is and smart monitoring for second homes.
