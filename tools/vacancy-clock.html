Insurance Vacancy Clock

Map your absence against your policy's vacancy clause and export the key dates.

Your absence

Enter your dates

    Not insurance advice

    Policies define vacancy and unoccupancy differently, and home watch visits do not necessarily make a home "occupied". This tool simply lays your dates against the number of days you enter so you can have an informed conversation with your agent. Read vacancy clauses explained for the questions to ask.

    The calendar file adds your departure, the day your stated limit is reached, each planned visit and your return to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
