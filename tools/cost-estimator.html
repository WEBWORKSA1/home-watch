How the estimate works

Most home watch providers charge a flat monthly fee set by how often they visit and how long each visit takes. The estimator starts with a typical per-visit range for your home size, adjusts it for property type and market, applies a small discount for frequent visits (and a minimum charge for monthly-only service), then adds the seasonal add-ons you choose.

Using the number

Treat the result as a budgeting range. Two providers with the same price can deliver very different value: compare visit length, checklist depth, report quality, insurance and response times. Our guide to home watch costs explains each factor, and 21 questions to ask helps you compare quotes.
