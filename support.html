Support Home.Watch

Home.Watch keeps its tools, guides and alerts free for every homeowner. Your support pays for the servers and data feeds, outreach to homeowners who need it, new writers and video creators, and the prizes in our monthly challenge.

Where support goes

35% ongoing operations — hosting, data feeds, tools and maintenance

25% promotion and marketing — reaching seasonal homeowners before they leave

20% hiring talent — writers, video creators and regional experts

20% contests and prizes — rewards for the community's best tips

You can direct your support to one purpose in the form. Home.Watch is an independent business, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

Other ways to help

  • Share a guide with a neighbor who is about to leave for the season
  • Enter the Away-Ready Home Challenge with your best tip
  • Sponsor a prize or a tool if you run a business
  • Tell us about an error or a topic we should cover through the contact form
